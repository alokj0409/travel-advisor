# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss the work, not the person. Give clear feedback, assume good intent, and make room for people with different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces used to represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the [alokj0409 GitHub profile](https://github.com/alokj0409). Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.
